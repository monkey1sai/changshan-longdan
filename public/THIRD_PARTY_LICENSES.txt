Zhao Yun character base model
----------------------------
Author: Quaternius
Pack: Universal Base Characters, Standard edition / Superhero Male
Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
Download: https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The humanoid geometry, UVs, original skin weights and eye/normal textures
come from this CC0 pack. Armour, helmet, costume materials and plume were
added for this game in Blender. The runtime GLB embeds its textures and
does not contact the source website.
